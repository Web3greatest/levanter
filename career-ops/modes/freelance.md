# Mode: freelance — Find and evaluate fast-income freelance gigs

## Trigger
`/career-ops freelance`
`/career-ops freelance {specific gig type}`

## Goal
Find freelance gigs that can generate income QUICKLY — within 1-4 weeks.
Minimum: ₦50,000 per project OR ₦30,000/month retainer.

## Step 1: Identify gig types to hunt

Based on profile, search for these gig categories:

**Events (fastest ROI):**
- Corporate event planning
- Wedding/social event coordination
- Product launch management
- Conference/seminar management
- Fashion show coordination
- Brand activations

**Fashion & Lifestyle:**
- Styling sessions (personal/commercial)
- Fashion shoot coordination
- Brand lookbook planning
- Wardrobe consulting
- Influencer event management

**Content & Marketing:**
- Social media management retainer
- Content calendar creation
- Brand photography/video direction
- Press release writing
- Email newsletter management

**Business Consulting:**
- Brand strategy session
- Market entry consulting
- Business plan review
- Workshop facilitation

## Step 2: Search platforms

Search these in priority order:

1. **Nigerian freelance platforms:**
   - Asuqu (https://www.asuqu.com) — search by category
   - Workaholic Nigeria — post your services
   - Fiverr.com — create service listings

2. **LinkedIn (Nigerian market):**
   - Search: "[gig type] Lagos freelance"
   - Post availability status
   - Message relevant company HR or managers directly

3. **Jobberman contract section:**
   - Filter: Contract/Freelance | Lagos | Industry

4. **Direct outreach targets:**
   - Lagos event companies (list from profile)
   - Fashion brands with in-house events
   - PR agencies on project basis
   - Startups needing one-time brand work

5. **International freelance (USD):**
   - Upwork: Events, Fashion, Marketing categories
   - Contra: Creative professionals
   - LinkedIn Remote Contract postings

## Step 3: Evaluate each gig

Quick score (3-point scale for freelance):

| Factor | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|--------|---|---|---|
| Pay | ≥₦100k/project | ₦50-99k | <₦50k |
| Timeline | Pays in <2 weeks | 2-4 weeks | 4+ weeks |
| Fit | Exactly in my skills | Adjacent | Stretch |

Threshold: Score ≥6 = recommend | 4-5 = flag | <4 = skip

## Step 4: Generate freelance pitch (not cover letter)

Freelance pitches are shorter and more direct than job cover letters:

**Structure (150-200 words):**
- Line 1: What you do + relevant credential
- Line 2: Specific to THEIR need (show you read their brief)
- Lines 3-5: 2-3 proof points (past events/projects)
- Line 6: Clear offer (what you'll deliver, rough timeline)
- Line 7: Call to action (schedule a call, review portfolio)

Save to `output/pitch-[client]-[project]-[date].md`

## Step 5: Build retainer pipeline

Identify 3-5 companies that could become monthly retainer clients:
- Social media management: ₦30k-₦80k/month
- Event consultant on retainer: ₦50k-₦150k/month
- Brand consultant: ₦40k-₦100k/month

For each potential retainer client:
1. Research them
2. Identify their gap (no in-house events person, inconsistent SM, etc.)
3. Pitch the retainer value (ongoing vs one-time)

## Step 6: Log and update tracker

Add each gig pursued to `data/tracker.md` with tag [FREELANCE].
