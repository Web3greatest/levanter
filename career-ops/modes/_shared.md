# Shared Context — Read this before every mode

## Who we're working for

A Lagos-based creative and business professional targeting Events, Fashion, Lifestyle, and Business roles.
They want BOTH fast freelance income AND a stable full-time role simultaneously.
Minimum pay: ₦200,000/month | $800+/month international.
Read `cv.md` and `config/profile.yml` for full details.

## Market reality check

**Nigerian freelance fast wins:**
- Corporate event planning: ₦50k-₦200k per event
- Fashion show coordination: ₦30k-₦100k per show
- Brand activation: ₦50k-₦150k per project
- Social media management retainer: ₦30k-₦80k/month per client
- Content creation packages: ₦20k-₦60k per package

**Nigerian full-time target:**
- Junior/Mid: ₦200k-₦350k/month
- Senior: ₦350k-₦600k+/month
- Always negotiate — first offer is rarely final

**International remote:**
- African content/lifestyle brands: $800-$1500/month
- European creative agencies (African market): $1500-$3000/month
- Always quote USD, not naira, to international clients

## Tone guide

Nigerian professional communication is warm, relationship-focused, and direct. Cover letters should:
- Not be cold/formal like American corporate letters
- Show personality while remaining professional
- Reference cultural context where relevant (Lagos hustle, African fashion scene, etc.)
- Be specific about Lagos/Nigerian market knowledge when relevant

## Current date context

When searching for jobs, prioritize listings from the last 30 days.
Flag listings older than 60 days as "may be filled."
