# Career-Ops Quick Start Guide

## Step 1: Fill in your CV (REQUIRED)

Edit `career-ops/cv.md` — replace all placeholder text with your actual:
- Professional summary
- Work experience (with dates, companies, achievements)
- Skills
- Education
- Notable projects

The more detailed your CV, the better your cover letters will be.

## Step 2: Fill in your profile (REQUIRED)

Edit `career-ops/config/profile.yml` — update:
- Your name, email, phone
- Your specific target roles
- Your actual skills list
- Companies you admire

## Step 3: Start using career-ops

In Claude Code (from the `/home/user/levanter` directory):

```
/career-ops              → See the menu and pipeline status

/career-ops scan         → Search job boards for open roles

/career-ops freelance    → Find fast-income gig opportunities
```

Or just **paste any job URL** — career-ops will auto-evaluate it.

## Step 4: Apply to roles

After evaluation, if grade is B or A:
```
/career-ops apply [company] [role]
```
This gives you:
1. A personalized cover letter to review
2. CV keyword audit
3. Application checklist
4. Follow-up timeline

**YOU submit the application manually** — career-ops prepares, you send.

## Step 5: Track your pipeline

```
/career-ops tracker
```
See all your applications, what needs action, and follow-up dates.

## FAQs

**Q: Can career-ops submit applications for me?**
A: No. It prepares everything but you always review and submit yourself. This protects you from AI errors.

**Q: What if salary isn't listed?**
A: Career-ops flags it. You decide whether to apply and will negotiate later.

**Q: Can I apply for both freelance and full-time at the same company?**
A: Usually yes — career-ops can prepare both a project pitch and a full-time application where relevant.

**Q: How do I update my application status?**
A: Tell Claude: "Mark [Company] as APPLIED" or "I got an interview at [Company]"
