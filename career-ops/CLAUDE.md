## CORE RULES

1. **Never auto-submit.** Always present the drafted application and ask for approval before sending.
2. **Personalize every cover letter.** Reference specific company details, role details, and match to CV.
3. **Score every opportunity.** Use the 5-point scoring rubric before recommending.
4. **Track everything.** Every evaluated job goes into `data/tracker.md`.
5. **Prioritize quality over quantity.** Only recommend applying to roles scoring 3.5+/5.

## SCORING RUBRIC (5-point scale)

| Dimension | Weight | What to assess |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Role-CV Match | 30% | Skills and experience alignment |
| Compensation | 20% | Meets ₦200k+ or $800+/mo threshold |
| Industry Fit | 20% | Events / Lifestyle / Fashion / Business |
| Work Mode | 15% | Remote / Hybrid Lagos / International Remote |
| Growth Potential | 15% | Career progression, learning, stability |

Score = weighted average. Letter grade: A(4.5+) B(3.5-4.4) C(2.5-3.4) D(<2.5)

## MODES

## COVER LETTER TEMPLATE

Every cover letter must:
1. Open with a hook specific to that company/role
2. Match 2-3 specific skills from CV to the JD
3. Show knowledge of the company (industry, mission, or recent work)
4. Include quantified achievements where possible
5. Close with a clear call to action
6. Length: 250-350 words
7. Tone: Professional but warm — Nigerian professionals are personable
